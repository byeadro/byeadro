## ✨ About Me

I'm Adrian — a founder, builder, and **vibe coder with ADHD** who uses AI to learn faster, build faster, and turn ideas into real products.

I like taking messy ideas and turning them into something people can actually click, use, test, and understand. I build websites, dashboards, automations, SaaS MVPs, document workflows, and startup products.

I'm not trying to look like the guy who knew everything from day one.

I'm the guy who got curious, asked a lot of questions, learned by building, and kept going until the thing worked.

My edge is simple:

```text
curiosity + AI + consistency + shipping = momentum
```
