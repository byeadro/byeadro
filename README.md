## ✨ About Me

I’m Adrian — a builder who fell in love with using AI to learn, create, and ship.

I like taking ideas from **“this would be cool”** to something real people can actually use.  
Most of what I build lives somewhere between:

- 🤖 AI tools  
- 🚀 SaaS MVPs  
- 🌐 websites and landing pages  
- 📊 dashboards and internal tools  
- 📄 OCR / document workflows  
- 🧠 startup experiments turned into real products  

I move fast, ask a lot of questions, learn by doing, and genuinely enjoy the process of figuring things out.

## ⚡ What I’m Building

### ⚖️ Embra
A student-first platform for law schools focused on mentorship, milestones, wellness, bar guidance, and student support.

### 🧠 BxB
A software and AI studio helping small businesses build websites, dashboards, internal tools, and custom automations.

### ⚡ Utility Bill Intelligence Platform
A system for extracting, structuring, and analyzing electric, gas, and water bill data from messy PDFs.

### 🤖 Claude Execution Engine
A deterministic Python pipeline that turns unstructured input into structured, validated Claude-ready prompts.
